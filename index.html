Nida

A story of dreams, courage, and love.

Nida, a girl from Ranchi, full of ambition and grace, dreams of making a mark in journalism. Her journey is not just about education but about standing tall with her beliefs.

She believes in love that respects freedom, and she values those who value her growth.

Every smile hides stories, and every picture tells one. This is just a glimpse of a beautiful soul.

In the quiet moments, she dreams loudest — of Delhi, of media, and of love that supports her wings.
